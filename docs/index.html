FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about emergency dental care and dental trauma repair.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out or loose tooth, a broken or chipped tooth, swelling or a suspected abscess, uncontrolled bleeding and injuries to the mouth or jaw are all dental emergencies. If you are unsure, call us and we will help you decide.

What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?

Pick the tooth up by the crown (not the root), gently rinse it if dirty without scrubbing, and try placing it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then call us immediately. Acting within the first hour gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

Do you offer same day and after hours appointments?

Yes. We provide same day and after hours emergency dental care. Call 616-221-6232 and we will arrange the fastest appointment available.

What does dental trauma repair involve?

Treatment depends on the injury. It may include re-positioning or stabilizing a tooth, bonding or restoring a chipped or broken tooth, root canal treatment, or an extraction if the tooth cannot be saved. We explain every option before we begin.

Is emergency dental care affordable?

We aim to keep urgent care accessible. Contact us to discuss your situation and what to expect, and we will be upfront about your options before treatment.
